# Rebalance the Homepage Color System

## Goal
Apply the approved 50% black, 30% gold, 20% white visual weighting while preserving the current layout, typography, card behavior, and button feel.

## Changes
- Make black the dominant structural color across the hero and major content bands.
- Keep white for readable content areas, card surfaces, and contrast.
- Increase gold’s visual presence through buttons, icon treatments, borders, highlights, dividers, and small decorative details—never large gold backgrounds.
- Remove or reduce the pastel color blocks that dilute the black–gold–white identity.
- Adjust card, testimonial, product, newsletter, and footer-adjacent treatments so the full page feels consistently premium.
- Preserve accessible contrast and the existing desktop/mobile interactions.

## Validation
- Check the homepage at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Confirm buttons, links, cards, text contrast, and decorative accents remain clear and balanced.
- Confirm the preview builds without errors.
